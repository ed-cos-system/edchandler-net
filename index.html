About Ed

The stories and the systems
behind them.

Ed Chandler accumulated nearly 3,000 flight hours and achieved more than 500 carrier arrested landings in four strike fighter aircraft. A TOPGUN graduate and combat-proven leader, he instructed and trained the best tactical thinkers and leaders in the world at the Navy's premier Air Warfare Development Command. With nearly 30 years of experience inside high-stakes teams and organizations, doing the hard work of leading difficult organizational change from within — he brings the stories and the systems behind them to your stage.
