About

Thirty years of
high-stakes decisions.

I flew F-14s and F/A-18s off carrier decks, went to TOPGUN, briefed Admirals in combat, and led squadrons through the kind of complexity that doesn't forgive bad decisions. When I transitioned out of uniform, I brought that operating system with me.

Today I work with executives, boards, and leadership teams who are navigating uncertainty, scaling fast, or trying to build organisations that perform when it matters most. My advisory practice is built on a proprietary leadership framework called DRIFT — and on the conviction that most organisations already have the talent they need; what they're missing is the operating architecture to unlock it.

I sit on corporate boards, advise defence and security companies, and speak to audiences who want more than inspiration — they want a framework they can actually use.
